# Print-ready Olivia collage

## Outcome
Create a high-resolution version of the uploaded “One year of Olivia” print while keeping its 4:5 composition, all twelve photos, month labels, title, and botanical details in the same places. Deliver the finished image as a downloadable file.

## Approach
- Enlarge the existing 1080 × 1350 JPEG to 3840 × 4800 pixels (4× linear scale) without regenerating faces or lettering.
- Apply restrained sharpening and image cleanup, then export a lossless PNG tagged for 300 dpi printing.
- Inspect the full image and close crops of faces, lettering, and fine botanical lines for artifacts before delivery.

## Print-quality note
Upscaling preserves the artwork but cannot recover detail absent from the 1080 × 1350 original. At 300 dpi, the enlarged file is nominally 12.8 × 16 inches; the effective photographic detail still depends on the source. If the original twelve photos or editable collage become available, those can support a genuinely sharper large-format rebuild.
